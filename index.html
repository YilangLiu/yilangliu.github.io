Hi! I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Yale University, advised by Prof. Ian Abraham. My research develops sample-based control and robot learning methods for visual policies, legged locomotion, dexterous manipulation, and autonomous exploration.

Previously, I received my M.S. from Carnegie Mellon University and dual B.E./B.S. degrees from Chongqing University and the University of Cincinnati. I have also worked as a robotics intern at Dexmate Inc.
